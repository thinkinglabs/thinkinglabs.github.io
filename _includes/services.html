Consulting CTO

Over the years, I have interviewed a considerable number of technology organisations in the context of technology due diligence and advised technology leadership in IT delivery optimisation. Having observed what works and what fails across different scales, I bring a pragmatic perspective to organisational design.

Through extensive work with CTOs, VPs of Engineering, directors, and hands-on engineers, I help technology leaders align technology strategy with business goals. I am available as a Consulting or Interim CTO to guide organisations through transitions, bridge leadership gaps, and advise executives on building a high-performance engineering culture.

Everything is contextual. What works for one organisation, does not necessarily work well for another organisation. Therefore, I start by listening across departments — from sales, marketing and product to engineering — to identify bottlenecks and start improving from there.

IT Delivery Consultant

Based on the 20+ years of experience delivering revenue-generating IT systems, I help organisations optimise their IT delivery with higher quality, increased speed and reliability while reducing stress.

This involves adopting the practices that make Continuous Integration, Continuous Delivery and Platform Engineering to establish clear paved roads and safety guardrails for teams. It means adopting targeted technological and organisational changes as part of a broader strategic technology vision to achieve a sustainable and effective delivery process.

This goes beyond automating the delivery. Tooling is the easy part; the real challenge lies in shifting the organisational mindset, reducing batch sizes, establishing delivery flow and embedding team habits.

As there is no one-size-fits-all roadmap to change, which changes to adopt and in what sequence depends on the unique circumstances and constraints of the organisation. I help you identify which capabilities to prioritise to improve delivery cadence with lasting results.

It is a continuous improvement journey requiring commitment and perseverance, not a one-off project.

I am available for adhoc online coaching sessions at a rate of 325 EUR per hour (excl. VAT). Check my calendar, for availability, or reach out directly to discuss longer-term engagements.

Technical Coaching and Team Programming

Instead of balancing quality against speed, I believe, practice and teach that better quality is the primary mechanism to achieve faster, safer, and better deliveries.

I provide hands-on technical coaching to software and platform engineering teams, guiding them to adopt modern engineering standards, and enhance collaboration.

My approach is tailored to the specific needs of each team, ensuring sustainable improvements and long-term autonomy.

I consider Team Programming (Mobbing) and Pair Programming to be superior delivery options. These operating models eliminate knowledge risks, elevate team capabilities and collaboration, and result in better products that better satisfy customer needs.
